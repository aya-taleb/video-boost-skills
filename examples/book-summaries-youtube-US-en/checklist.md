# Checklist: book summaries on YouTube (long-form), US, English

Made 2026-10-23 · valid until 2026-11-23 · sources from the last 12 months

Levels: **must** (policy or strong evidence), **avoid** (known mistake), **should** (good evidence), **nice** (small or mixed evidence).

## Policy
- **must** Make every video its own work: its own script, pictures and structure. Never a fill-in template with only the names changed. *(why: YouTube does not pay for templated or mass-produced content, and it judges the whole channel.)* [yt-01]
- **must** No AI character presents as a doctor, lawyer or money expert giving advice. *(why: YouTube does not pay for AI personas giving health, legal or finance advice.)* [yt-02]
- **must** Summarize the book's ideas in your own words, keep quotes short, and credit the book and author on screen. *(why: A recap that replaces the book, or long excerpts, is high copyright risk.)* [yt-15]
- **nice** Turn on YouTube's AI label only when the video looks realistic. Clearly animated videos do not need it. *(why: Only realistic synthetic content needs the label.)* [yt-03]

## Hook
- **should** Open on the promise or payoff in the first seconds. No greeting, no slow intro. *(why: The share still watching at 30 seconds (Intros) is the first thing that drops; YouTube rewards satisfied viewers.)* [yt-08, yt-06]
- **should** Say or show the book's name in the first 15 seconds. *(why: Top book summary titles lead with the book; viewers should know at once they are in the right place.)* [yt-10]

## Story
- **should** Use a clear structure: numbered takeaways with a section card for each. *(why: Structured videos kept more viewers than talking-head videos in one 2026 channel study.)* [yt-09, yt-10]
- **avoid** Use the same format and visuals video after video until it feels repetitive. *(why: A big psychology channel's views fell with repetitive listicles; templated channels are not paid.)* [yt-13, yt-01]

## Length
- **should** For a full book summary, plan 30 to 50 minutes in one video. *(why: The top book summary channel runs about 49 minutes; long videos also build the 8,000 watch hours needed from 2027.)* [yt-10, yt-16]
- **nice** Keep one book in one video. Do not split it into parts. *(why: A 'Part 2' drew far fewer views on the top channel.)* [yt-11]

## Visuals
- **should** Thumbnail: a face (a character face counts as a face here) and about 5 words or fewer. *(why: Most breakout thumbnails in a 500-video 2026 study had a face; the median text was 5 words.)* [yt-14]
- **should** Test up to 3 titles and thumbnails with YouTube's A/B test. *(why: YouTube picks the winner by watch time.)* [yt-05]

## Text
- **should** Title: the exact book title plus a time promise (for example 'Atomic Habits in 30 Minutes'). *(why: This is the title pattern of the top book summary channel.)* [yt-10]
- **nice** Add chapters for videos over 5 minutes (first at 0:00, at least 3). *(why: Chapters need 0:00 and 3 timestamps to show.)* [yt-17]

## Engagement
- **avoid** Offer anything in return for likes, subscribes or comments. *(why: Asking is allowed; rewarding engagement is not.)* [yt-07]
- **should** End with a 5 to 20 second end screen that points to the next video, with room for up to 4 elements. *(why: End screens only run in the last 5 to 20 seconds; suggested videos are where most viewing comes from.)* [yt-04, yt-06]
- **should** Make it a series: name what comes next and link it. *(why: Viewers mostly find videos through suggested videos, not the subscription feed.)* [yt-06]

## Posting
- **nice** Choose quality over volume. *(why: The top book summary channel grew to 307K subscribers with 20 videos.)* [yt-10]
